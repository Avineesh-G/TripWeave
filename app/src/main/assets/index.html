1. Purpose of TripWeave

TripWeave is an autonomous road trip itinerary planner and route optimizer engineered for traveling across India. It helps you design multi-stop journeys, verifies operating hours for heritage monuments, checks daylight buffer times, and models accurate per-person fuel, toll, and homestay budgets.

2. Device Permissions Requested & Why

Device Location (GPS & Wi-Fi)

Requested solely while actively using the app to show your current location dot on the map, calculate exact driving distance to destinations, and recommend famous food, monuments, and parks around you. Your real-time location stays within your client device and is never tracked in the background or shared with third-party advertisers.

Local Storage & Offline Caching

Used to cache your active routes, vehicle mileage parameters, and map tile choices locally on your device for uninterrupted offline road trip planning on remote highways.

3. Privacy Protection & Cloud Security

  • Google Authentication Only: We authenticate users strictly through Google OAuth 2.0. We never request, store, or process user passwords.
  • Encrypted Cloud Sync: Your saved trips and expense breakdowns are saved in Google Cloud Firestore under your private authenticated user document (users/{user_id}/trips), accessible only by your verified Google account.
  • Zero Ad Trackers: TripWeave contains zero commercial ad trackers, marketing cookies, or third-party data analytics brokers.

4. Map Guidelines & Road Safety Advisory

TripWeave utilizes official Google Maps layers adhering to standard official map compliance and national road guidelines.

Road Safety Advisory: Travel plans, visiting hours, and fuel cost models are decision-support estimates. Drivers must always adhere to local traffic rules, speed limits, and official road conditions.
